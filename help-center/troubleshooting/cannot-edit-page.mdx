---
title: Cannot Edit a Documentation Page
description: Quick fix if you can't edit a TWG doc page.
---

## Symptoms

- "Edit" button is disabled
- Changes are not saving

## Causes

- Insufficient user permissions
- Page is locked by another editor

## Solution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check Your Role">
    Only Editors/Admins can modify pages. Ask your admin to update your role in the Team settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Page Locked?">
    If another user is editing, wait or ask them to close their editing session.
  </Step>
</Steps>
