## Overview

Collaborate by adding users to your team.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Settings">
    In the dashboard sidebar, select "Team".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Member">
    Click "Invite", enter their email, and pick a role (Viewer/Editor/Admin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invitation">
    Click "Send". The user gets an email with access instructions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
