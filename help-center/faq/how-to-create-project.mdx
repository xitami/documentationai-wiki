---
title: How do I create a new project?
description: Quick steps to create your first TWG project.
---

## Steps

1. Login to your TWG account.
2. Click "New Project" on the dashboard.
3. Enter your project name and optional description.
4. Select the team or workspace.
5. Click "Create" to finish.

## Tips

- You can invite team members after creation.
- Projects can be managed in the Projects tab.
